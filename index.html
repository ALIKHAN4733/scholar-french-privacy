Privacy Policy for Scholar French

Effective date: 18 October 2026 · Last updated: 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Scholar French mobile application (“Scholar French”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our app. By using Scholar French, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Who we are

Scholar French is an educational application that helps users learn the French language through songs, educational videos, and interactive word-matching mini-games. The app is published by Scholars (“we”). If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at aliashar456@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account information: when you create an account or sign in, we collect your name, email address, and a unique user ID. If you sign in through Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple, we receive the basic profile information these services share with us (name, email, profile picture, and a unique identifier).
  • Learning content and progress: we store your lesson progress, scores, saved songs, game results, and any content you create or save within the app so that your progress is available across sessions and devices.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: we collect limited technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language settings, time zone, approximate region, and crash diagnostics. This data helps us keep the app stable and improve the user experience.
  • Performance data: through Firebase Performance Monitoring, we automatically collect anonymised metrics about app startup time, screen rendering, and network request performance.

2.3 Information we do not collect

Scholar French does not collect your precise location, contacts, SMS messages, call logs, photos, microphone or camera input, financial information, or health information.

3. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you across devices.
  • To save your learning progress, scores, and preferences.
  • To deliver songs, videos, and games to you and personalise the learning experience.
  • To fix crashes and bugs, improve app performance, and develop new features.
  • To communicate important service updates (for example, security notices).
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms of service.

4. Legal basis for processing (users in the EEA / UK)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Performance of a contract: to provide the app’s core features once you create an account.
  • Legitimate interests: to improve the app, keep it secure, and prevent abuse.
  • Consent: where required, for optional features such as analytics; you can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable law.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with the following categories of recipients, strictly for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Service providers that help us run the app:
    • Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Performance Monitoring) — hosting, authentication, data storage, and performance monitoring. See Firebase Privacy and Security.
    • Google Sign-In — identity verification when you choose to sign in with Google.
    • Apple Sign-In — identity verification when you choose to sign in with Apple.
    • Google Fonts — font delivery.
  • Legal and safety: we may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or the public.
  • Business transfers: if the app is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred, subject to this policy or an equivalent one.

6. Data retention

We retain your account information and learning progress for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete associated personal data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to keep certain records (for example, financial or security logs), or where the data has been de-identified so that it can no longer be linked to you.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your account and your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at aliashar456@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable law.

8. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time by emailing aliashar456@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. We will process the request within 30 days. An in-app account-deletion option is also available in the app settings.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from a child under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in the relevant jurisdiction) without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data without consent, please contact us so we can delete it.
